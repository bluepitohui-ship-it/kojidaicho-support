What it does

From the first photo to the finished report, in one app.

A photo board on every shot

A small board with the project name, date and notes is placed on each photo, so months later the picture still says what it shows. The compass heading can be recorded too.

PDF reports on the spot

Make a report with a cover page in one tap, then email it or print it. Choose A4 or Letter paper, and one to three photos per page.

Shot lists catch missed photos

List the photos you need — before, in progress, completed — and the app tells you which ones are still missing.

Pick an item, then shoot

Choose a shot-list item on the camera screen and each photo is filed under it. Afterwards, swipe right to keep a photo and left to skip it.

How photos are handled

Site photos are your clients' information.

  • This app does not send photos or project info anywhere. It connects to the network only to buy or restore Pro (Apple App Store).
  • There is no server for this app. The developer cannot see your photos or project info.
  • There is no account, so no email address or password is collected.
  • There are no ads and no usage tracking.
  • Photos leave the app only when you share, print or back them up yourself.

Read the Privacy Policy

Please note  The board in this app is a simple image composited onto the photo. It has no tamper detection and is not certified to any official standard. Deleting the app also deletes the photos inside it, so keep a backup (iCloud Backup, a copy in the Photos app, or a per-project backup — see Settings → Protect your photos).
