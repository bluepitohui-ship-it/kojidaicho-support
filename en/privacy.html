Privacy Policy

Site Photo Log (iPhone app)　Last updated: November 27, 2026

Information this app collects

This app does not collect personal information. It never sends photos, project info or usage records to servers run by the developer or any third party.

Photos and project info

The photos you take, board contents, project info, company name and logo are stored only on your iPhone. They are handed over only when you choose "Send / Save", "Back up" or "Print", and only to the destination you choose.

Camera and photo access

The camera is used only to take jobsite photos. From the Photos app, only the photos you pick are imported into a project.

Photos copy and iPhone backup

If you turn it on in Settings, a copy of each photo you take is also saved to the iPhone Photos app. The permission used is "Add Photos Only"; the app never reads the photos in your library. iCloud Backup and the Photos app are features of the iPhone and store data in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it.

Information inside shared files

Photos in reports (PDF, Excel, Word), backups and Photos copies do not carry embedded location data. Location appears only as visible text, and only if you choose to show "Position" on the board or in the caption area.

Location and heading

Location and the compass are used only for the compass guide and for "Position" and "Heading" on the board and in reports. You are asked for permission the first time you use these features. Everything else works without it. The position and heading are stored only on your iPhone as part of the photo record and are not sent to the developer or any third party (they are included in backup files). If you give someone a photo with "Position" on the board, or a report showing position, they can tell where it was taken.

Network use

This app makes no network connections for ads, analytics or automatic crash reports. It connects to the Apple App Store only when you buy or restore Pro (payment details are handled by Apple and never reach the developer).

Deleting data

Deleting the app erases all photos and info inside it. Back up important projects first.
